# Offer Skill

Rules the agent follows when it drafts offers.

## Each offer must

1. Name one concrete outcome for one specific ICP, in the words that ICP uses.
2. Be narrow enough that the buyer knows at a glance it is for them.
3. Rest on the selected case studies and the company's real capabilities. Never invent clients, numbers or results that are not in the source material.
4. Have a clear promise (one or two sentences), 3-5 deliverables and a realistic timeline.
5. Take a different angle from the other offers in the batch.

## Avoid

- Generic service labels ("AI Automation", "Web Development").
- Hype words and vague claims without a result behind them.
