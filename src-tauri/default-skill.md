# Content Ideas Skill

Generate high-performing social content ideas with validated hooks and consistent quality.

## Each idea must

1. Use a DIFFERENT hook style from the other ideas in the batch (practical, contrarian, story-based, data-driven).
2. Be substantial: real depth, specific details, data when the source material has it.
3. Sound conversational and authentic, never salesy.
4. Tie back to the focus offer's promise, the ICP's pain, or a case study result. Never invent client names, numbers, or results that are not in the source material.
5. End the draft with a CTA, varied across ideas:
   - Question: "What's your experience with X?"
   - Challenge: "I'd love for someone to tell me I'm wrong."
   - Soft offer: one line pointing at the focus offer.

**Style:** opinion-driven, first-person. Separators: dashes, bullets, arrows (->). Minimal emoji. Short paragraphs.

## No false-contrast filler (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

NEVER use the "isn't X / it's Y" or "not about X / it's about Y" pattern. Say what you mean directly.

Bad: "This isn't about automation. It's about leverage."
Good: "This is about leverage."

Bad: "The goal isn't just to make my team more efficient. It's to make them more technical."
Good: "The goal is to make my team more efficient and technical."

If you catch yourself writing "isn't/not [something]. It's [the real point]", delete the first sentence and lead with the real point.

## Hook rules (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Every title/hook MUST pass both tests:

**1. The Number Test** - at least one specific number ($76M, 90 days, 15-person team, 70-80%).

Bad: "AI SDRs are failing"
Good: "AI SDRs raised $200M+ and 70-80% of implementations fail by month 3"

**2. The Visualization Test** - the reader can picture a specific scene. "Can I see this in my head?"

Bad: "Sales teams struggle with outbound"
Good: "I had coffee with a VP of Sales last week who's never heard of Clay"

If a hook fails either test, rewrite it before answering.

## Hook templates

Use as inspiration and fill with specifics from the offer, ICP and case studies.

- `It's ridiculous that [blank]`
- `I have spent an absurd amount of time [blank], here is something that will [blank]`
- `Why is nobody talking about what [blank] just did`
- `You know, I used to think that [blank] but then I realised...`
- `Everybody says "[blank]" when trying [blank] but nobody gives you a solid framework to [get outcome]`
- `If I had [X] days to make [$XX] using [blank] here's exactly how I'd do it`
- `This is the weirdest [statistic/fact/result] I think I've ever seen when it comes to [blank]`
- `I hate to break it to you but [blank]`
- `If you're still doing [blank] in [year] you're wasting money/time`
- `No one cares about [blank] anymore. Here's what you should do going forward`
- `I messed up...`
- `If you're not obsessively focused on these 3 things you can forget about [blank]`
- `This is how to get [result] from your [vehicle] without [hard thing]`
- `I [insert activity] in the last [X] days and here's 4 things I learned`
- `This is a controversial opinion - don't get mad at me but [say the thing]`
- `I'm a [job title] and this is exactly how I [get result]`
- `Don't [action] until you've done this one thing`
- `Everyone tells you to [common saying] but they never show you how. Here's a 3-step framework that works for me.`
- `6 months ago I was [successful thing] and then I realised that I was [downside] so I decided to [blank]`
- `Here's how one of our clients generated [$XX] in [timeframe] using a [blank] strategy`
- `If you're [doing X], you should [do Y]`
- `[Company/Tool] promised [X]. I'm looking around, and all I see is [Y].`
- `I [did something] with [person/type] who's never heard of [thing you assumed everyone knew]`

## Reference examples

### Practical / educational
```
If you're running ABM, you should start scoring awareness.

Unlike other RevOps systems, reps LOVE this.

It answers one question: "Does this account actually know we exist?"
```

### Contrarian / opinion
```
Salesforce promised us an AI revolution with Agentforce. I'm looking around, and all I see is a whole lot of nothing.

The $300B giant is stumbling.

-> Technical glitches.
-> Slow adoption.
-> Executives quietly backpedaling on their "agentic AI" promises.
```

### Story / data-driven
```
I had coffee with a VP of Sales last week who's never heard of Clay.

This is a guy running a 15-person SDR team at a Series B SaaS company doing $20M ARR.

Smart operator. Reads all the sales blogs. Goes to conferences.
```
